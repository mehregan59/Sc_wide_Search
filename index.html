About SWD Search

Purpose

SWD Search systematically collects and structures global occurrence, distribution, and strain-origin records of Drosophila suzukii (Matsumura, 1931). It queries heterogeneous sources in parallel, extracts location data, classifies evidence, and exports for mapping, meta-analysis, and systematic review.

Schema editor

The Schema tab lets you control exactly what information is collected and how it appears. You can disable fields you don't need, rename columns to your preferred terminology, edit the keyword lists used for automatic extraction, and add entirely new custom fields. All exports — CSV, GeoJSON, and the schema JSON itself — update immediately to reflect your choices.

Output categories

A Primary location records
Original collection, trapping, detection, sequencing, or monitoring.
B Useful sampling locations
Confirmed presence but not first records.
C Lab / experimental strain origin
Geographic origin of laboratory colonies or field material.
D Modelling / review
Secondary geographic information from models and reviews.
E No usable location
Mentions the species but no extractable location.
F Pre-1980 historical records
Historical and native-range records before 1980.

APIs used

All APIs are public and free: OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Crossref, GBIF, iNaturalist, Zenodo. Institutional databases (EPPO, CABI, USDA, NARO, CAAS, RDA) are stubbed — see js/app.js to add connectors.
