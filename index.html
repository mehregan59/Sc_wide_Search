Paywalled DOIs

Every paper found behind a paywall, with DOI and direct access options.

How to get these papers legally:
1. Email the author — find on Google Scholar or ResearchGate. Authors almost always respond.
2. Interlibrary loan (ILL) — your university library, free, usually 24–48 hours.
3. Unpaywall — click the Unpaywall link to check for a legal free version automatically.
4. Europe PMC — checks for author manuscripts and preprints.

About SciWide Search

♥ Support this project

SciWide Search is free to use and will stay that way. It queries multiple academic and biodiversity databases at no cost to you. If it saved you time on a literature search, a small donation helps cover the time spent maintaining it, fixing things when external APIs change, and building the features people ask for.

Purpose

SciWide Search is an open-source tool for systematically collecting and structuring records from academic literature, grey literature, and biodiversity databases. It is designed to support PRISMA-style systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and global distribution mapping across any research topic. Enter any search terms — it is not limited to any specific organism or field.

Schema & customisation

The Schema tab lets you control exactly which fields are collected. Rename columns, toggle fields on or off, edit the keyword lists used for automatic extraction, and add entirely new custom fields. All exports update immediately to reflect your schema. Save your configuration as a preset and share it with collaborators.
