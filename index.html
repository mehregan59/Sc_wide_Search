About SWD Search

Purpose

SWD Search is an open-source tool for systematically collecting and structuring global occurrence, distribution, and strain-origin records of Drosophila suzukii (Matsumura, 1931) — the spotted wing drosophila. It queries heterogeneous sources in parallel, extracts location data, classifies evidence type, and exports to formats suitable for mapping, meta-analysis, and systematic review.

Search strategy

Queries are broad and multilingual. Sources are not excluded because the title or abstract lacks geographic terms — all papers mentioning the species are captured and flagged for manual full-text review of methods sections, sampling site descriptions, and supplementary materials.

Laboratory and experimental studies are included if they report the geographic origin of fly strains or colonies. One row is created per source-location combination, so a single paper reporting multiple sites produces multiple rows.

Output categories

A Primary location records
Original collection, trapping, detection, sequencing, or monitoring.
B Useful sampling locations
Not first records, but additional confirmed presence sites.
C Lab / experimental strain origin
Geographic origin of laboratory colonies or field material used in experiments.
D Modelling / review
Secondary geographic information from models and review papers.
E No usable location
Mentions D. suzukii but contains no extractable location data.
F Pre-1980 historical records
Historical and native-range records from before 1980.

APIs used

All APIs are public and free: Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Crossref, GBIF, iNaturalist, Zenodo. Rate limits are respected automatically via built-in delays. Institutional databases (EPPO, CABI, USDA, NARO, CAAS, RDA) are stubbed — see js/engines.js to add real connectors.
