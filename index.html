Purpose

SciWide Search is an open-source tool for systematically collecting and structuring records from academic literature, grey literature, and biodiversity databases. It supports PRISMA-style systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and global distribution mapping across any research topic.

Workflow

1. Configure a search and define your search parameters as Requirements (use the AI synonym button to widen them). 2. Run the search — requirements are checked automatically afterward, with a full-text fallback for open-access records. 3. Optionally use AI Extraction to hand off DOIs to a browsing-capable AI for a second pass. 4. Review everything in Results (nothing is ever removed, only flagged) and download from Export.

Output categories

A Primary location records
Original collection, trapping, detection, sequencing, or monitoring.
B Useful sampling locations
Confirmed presence but not first records.
C Lab / experimental strain origin
Geographic origin of laboratory colonies or field material.
D Modelling / review
Secondary geographic information from models and reviews.
E No usable location
Mentions the topic but no extractable location.
F Pre-1980 historical records
Historical records before 1980.
