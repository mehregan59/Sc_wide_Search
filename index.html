Extraction slots

What are extraction slots? Each slot scans every record’s title, abstract, and excerpt for the phrases you define, and records which ones matched. Results appear as extra columns in the Results table and as separate CSV exports after a search. Up to 10 slots — hover the ? guide button on any slot for field-specific examples and tips.

Tip: use specific multi-word phrases rather than single common words to reduce false matches. Toggle “Partial match” for compound terms or word stems.

About SciWide Search

Purpose

SciWide Search is an open-source tool for systematically collecting and structuring records from academic literature, grey literature, and biodiversity databases. It supports PRISMA-style systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and global distribution mapping across any research topic. Enter any search terms — it is not limited to any specific organism or field.

Systematic review support

The tool is designed to fit a full PRISMA workflow. The Search log export produces a methods-ready query record. The Screen column lets you flag records Include / Exclude / Maybe with a free-text reason. Extraction slots let you scan every abstract for custom phrase lists. The Export tab organises all outputs into five groups.

Output categories

A Primary location records
Original collection, trapping, detection, sequencing, or monitoring.
B Useful sampling locations
Confirmed presence but not first records.
C Lab / experimental strain origin
Geographic origin of laboratory colonies or field material.
D Modelling / review
Secondary geographic information from models and reviews.

Historical records before 1980.
