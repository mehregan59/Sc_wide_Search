Purpose

SciWide Search is an open-source tool for systematically collecting and structuring records from academic literature, grey literature, and biodiversity databases. It supports PRISMA-style systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and global distribution mapping across any research topic. Enter any search terms — it is not limited to any specific organism or field.

Systematic review support

The tool is designed to fit a full PRISMA workflow. The Search log export produces a methods-ready query record showing every database queried, every search term, hit counts, and deduplication outcomes. The Screen column in Results lets you flag each record as Include, Exclude, or Maybe with a free-text reason. Included and excluded sets export separately as CSV. All screening decisions are also embedded in the CSV, JSON, and RIS exports.

Mentions the topic but no extractable location.

Historical records before 1980.
